Exit Criteria

The 4 Pillars

Document at least 2 of the 4 criteria before creating an Opportunity. All 4 gives the AE the strongest possible starting point.

📋
The minimum: 2 of 4 criteria completed before creating an Opportunity in Salesforce. Push for all 4 where you can. If you only have 2, make sure they are specific and well-documented.
1

Decision Maker

Who can say yes

A named contact with real influence over the buying decision. Doesn't need to be the final signer, but must have ownership over the payment or finance problem you are solving.

2

Pain

The specific problem driving change

A documented operational or financial challenge, in the prospect's own words where possible. "Unhappy with their current provider" is a starting point, not a finish line.

3

Timeline

When they want to act

A defined readiness window based on what they told you. Never guess. Always ask directly. Sets the AE's expectations for deal velocity before they dial.

4

PSA and Accounting Software

Their current tech stack

Document their PSA and accounting platforms (e.g. ConnectWise, QuickBooks, NetSuite). Confirm or update these fields in Salesforce based on discovery or pre-call research.

Pillar 1

Decision Maker

Identify at least one person with real influence over the buying decision. Doesn't have to be the final signer, but it needs to be someone with ownership over the payment or finance problem.

⚠️
If you select Other in the DM field, include a specific name and title in your BDR Exit Notes. Leaving it blank will be flagged in the weekly quality review.

💬 Discovery Questions

"Who on your team ultimately owns decisions around payment processing or AP/AR?"
"If you found a solution that solved this, who would need to sign off?"
"Beyond yourself, who else would be in the room when a decision like this gets made?"
"Is this something your team could move on independently, or does it need sign-off from finance or the C-suite?"
Pillar 2

Pain

The specific operational or financial challenge driving the prospect's willingness to change. The more specific, the more useful for the AE going into Discovery.

🔍 Pain Categories and What Good Looks Like

High processing fees
"Paying about 2.9% on all card transactions. CFO wants to reduce by 40+ bps."
Manual reconciliation
"Reconciling 3,000+ transactions per month manually in Excel. Two FTEs dedicated to it."
Poor acceptance rates
"Decline rates around 8 to 12%. Losing $200K+ in revenue monthly."
Fraud and chargebacks
"Experiencing a 1.5% chargeback rate. At risk of processor review."
Limited payment methods
"Can't accept ACH or international cards. Losing enterprise deals because of it."
Integration gaps
"Current solution doesn't connect with NetSuite. Team doing manual data entry every day."

💬 Discovery Questions

"What's your biggest frustration with your current payment process?"
"Can you walk me through how reconciliation works today? Where does it break down?"
"Have you looked into solving this before? What happened?"
"If you could fix one thing about your payments setup tomorrow, what would it be?"
"What does this problem cost you in time, money, or deals you are not winning?"
Pillar 3

Timeline

Always ask directly. A warm conversation does not always mean an active project. Never assume based on how the call felt.

📅 Timeline Stages

Immediate0 to 30 days
Active evaluation underway. AE prioritizes and targets a demo within 5 business days of handoff.
Short-term1 to 3 months
Defined initiative, likely current or start of next quarter. AE drives urgency.
Mid-term3 to 6 months
Known project, delayed start. AE qualifies on what trigger would accelerate the timeline.
Long-term6 to 12 months
Exploratory but engaged. AE nurtures and sets a future touch cadence. Still worth documenting.
Exploratory12+ months
No defined project yet. AE takes the call with expectations managed. May return to BDR nurture.
No Timeline
Prospect was unclear or deflected. A few more questions can often turn this into a real timeline.

💬 Discovery Questions

"Is there a specific date or quarter you are targeting to have something new in place?"
"What would need to happen for this to move forward in the next 30 to 60 days?"
"Are there external deadlines driving this? Contract renewals, audits, system migrations?"
"On a scale of 1 to 10, how urgent is solving this right now versus 6 months from now?"
"Is this more of a someday thing, or is there actual urgency behind it for your team right now?"
Step-by-Step

Creating a Qualified Opportunity

Follow these steps every time you create an Opportunity in Salesforce.

1
Run the qualification call Use the discovery questions in this playbook. Ask directly. Even a few specific answers are more useful than vague impressions.
2
Complete at least 2 of 4 exit criteria fields Fill in Decision Maker, Pain, Timeline, and/or PSA and Accounting Software in Salesforce. If you have all four, great. Two solid ones is the minimum to advance.
3
Write your BDR Exit Notes Include the prospect's own words, any trigger events, objections you handled, competitors they mentioned, their tone, and the next step you agreed on. Aim for 400+ characters.
4
Create the Opportunity in Salesforce Set the stage to Qualified. Only create the Opportunity once criteria are documented. Do not fill fields retroactively.
5
Track AE acceptance The AE commits to accepting or acknowledging the handoff within 24 business hours. If you don't hear back, flag it to your manager.

🤝 What the AE Commits to When You Hand Off

  • ✓
    Read your notes before the call, not during it.
  • ✓
    Accept or dispute within 24 business hours of the Opportunity being created.
  • ✓
    Conduct Discovery within the SLA for the given timeline tier (5 days for Immediate).
  • ✓
    Use the context you provided. The prospect should not need to repeat themselves.
Your Notes

What Good BDR Exit Notes Look Like

Think of your notes as a briefing document for the AE. The goal is to help them walk into Discovery already understanding the prospect's world.

📝
Aim for 400+ characters. That's roughly 3 to 5 sentences of useful context. Strong notes are a signal of thorough qualification.

✅ What to Include

Trigger event
What is driving the urgency? Contract renewal, audit, system migration, new hire, org change.
Their own words
Quotes are gold. Write down verbatim anything memorable they said about their problem.
Objections you handled
What did they push back on and how did you address it? Saves the AE from walking in blind.
Competitors mentioned
Are they evaluating anyone else? Even a passing mention of who they use or have looked at is useful.
Tone and sentiment
Were they excited, cautious, just exploring? The AE needs to calibrate their approach before they call.
Agreed next step
What exactly did you tell the prospect happens next? The AE must honor this commitment.
Dispute Process

How Disputes Work

The dispute process handles disagreements about qualification. Most handoffs should never need it.

⚖️ Valid vs. Not Valid

Valid Grounds
DM field title does not match who the BDR actually spoke to
Documented pain cannot be verified with the prospect
Timeline marked Immediate but prospect confirms no active project
Contact is not at the right company or in a relevant role
Prospect explicitly said they were not interested and the meeting was misrepresented
Not Valid
"The prospect wasn't very warm" — discovery is the AE's job
"They have a competitor" — competition is normal
"They have a long timeline" — long-term is valid pipeline
"I don't think they'll close" — the AE's instinct doesn't invalidate a BDR's qualification
"The notes weren't detailed enough" — a coaching note, not a dispute

🔁 Dispute Workflow

1
AE files a disputeMarks the opportunity Disputed in Salesforce with written rationale. Must happen within 48 hours of Opportunity creation.
2
BDR respondsYou have 24 hours to provide supporting evidence: call notes, a recording, or the contact record.
3
Managers review jointlyBoth managers review within 48 hours. The outcome is: Uphold, Overturn, or Escalate.
4
Decision is communicatedBDR Manager shares the outcome in Salesforce and via Slack. Final unless escalated.
5
Escalation if neededEither party can escalate to VP of Sales within 24 hours of the decision, but only with credible new evidence. No further escalation beyond VP level.
Your Sourcing Credit

Protect Your Credit. Qualify Well.

Qualify thoroughly and document honestly. Your credit is protected. These provisions exist for edge cases where shortcuts create problems for the whole team.

The Philosophy

The goal is not to take money from BDRs. It is to make sure the pipeline we are all working from is real. A BDR who qualifies honestly will never face a clawback.

🛡️ Protections in Place for You

  • ✓
    Clawbacks cannot be initiated more than 30 days after the Discovery call (unless fraud is suspected)
  • ✓
    AEs cannot initiate a clawback unilaterally. Both managers must agree.
  • ✓
    You have 5 business days to respond with counter-evidence before a clawback is finalized.
  • ✓
    First-time issues with no prior coaching on record result in a warning, not a clawback.
  • ✓
    Partial clawbacks (25 to 50% of credit) may apply where criteria were met in spirit but documented imprecisely.

⚠️ Situations That Put Credit at Risk
